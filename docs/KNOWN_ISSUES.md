# Lumen — Known issues

Problems found and not fixed yet, with what they affect and where they are
addressed. Fixed issues move to the relevant decision record or manual test
run. Add new ones here as they are found.

| 2026-10-20 (M7 slice A5) | **A take and an edit of the same track make more than one undo step.** A MIDI take is committed about once a second as one step (`Record`); an edit made meanwhile, such as a fader move or a lane written by Touch, is a step of its own between the commits, so Undo takes the take back in pieces. The edit itself is no longer lost (`place` puts back only the track's clips, not the whole track), and clip edits made to the recording track during a take are still replaced by the commit | A history that merges a recording's commits across other steps |
| 2026-10-20 (M7 slice A5) | **A controller's moves are recorded at the playhead of the frame that applies them**, not on the message's own tick: the recorder samples a control once a frame, so a point can be up to a frame (about 16 ms) late, and several messages inside one frame become one value. The tap carries each message's device frame (`Heard::played`), unused so far. Not measured with a real controller | A recorder that takes timestamped samples; with MT-AUTO-6's accuracy numbers |
| 2026-10-20 (M7 slice A5) | **Only absolute controllers are mapped.** A knob that sends relative values (encoders) jumps the control to a meaningless value, and there is no pickup, so a control jumps to the knob's position at its first move. | 2026-10-20 (M7 slice A5) | **Mappings are not carried with a copied device or track**, and a mapping to a control that is gone stays in the list as "A control that is gone" until cleared | If it bites: pruning in the Document methods that remove tracks, sends and devices |
